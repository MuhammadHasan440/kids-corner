THE SPARK OF CURIOSITY

A Family Full of Curiosity

Behind Kidz Corner with Haven is Dani Egalite, a parent and creator turning everyday family moments into opportunities for learning, music, and imagination.

As a mother of four, Dani noticed that the moments when children learn best aren’t when they are being tested, but when they feel heard, encouraged, and excited to explore.

When creating Kidz Corner, 9-year-old Haven became her natural creative partner. Haven provides real-kid feedback, co-stars in learning videos, and brings an infectious enthusiasm for science, nursery rhymes, and song.

The creator’s first children’s book was inspired by Haven encountering a tough reading challenge and saying, “I can’t do it.” By adding the single word “YET,” Dani helped transform frustration into a growth mindset habit that now guides their entire educational brand.

Heartfelt Learning Every song and lesson comes from authentic home experiences.
Child-Led Curiosity Haven’s genuine wonder guides the topics we explore.
Growth Mindset Empowering kids to see mistakes as stepping stones to mastery.
