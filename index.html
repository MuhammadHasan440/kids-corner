THE INSPIRATION BEHIND THE BOOK

A Little Word. A Big Difference.

Inspired by a simple everyday moment at home, this story celebrates the power of perseverance, learning, and believing in what is possible.

“One afternoon, Haven was trying to sound out a difficult sentence and said, ‘Mom, I can’t read it.’ I gently smiled and whispered, ‘...YET.’ That single word transformed frustration into curiosity.” — Dani Egalite, Creator & Mother of Four

Every child encounters something they can’t do — until they keep learning. Inspired by this little moment at home, this children's book celebrates the power of "yet" and reminds young readers that growth takes time, patience, and practice.

  • ✓ Emotional Resilience: Helps children process frustration and replace defeat with optimism.
  • ✓ Parent & Child Bonding: Written with real language parents can weave into bedtime and homework routines.
  • ✓ Authentic Inspiration: Co-created alongside Haven’s real childhood questions and discoveries.

FOR PARENTS & EDUCATORS

Growing Together, One Little Lesson at a Time

Parenting is full of little moments that become big lessons. Kidz Corner is here to make learning a little more joyful, one song and story at a time.

What is your little learner excited to discover?

Here are three simple ways Dani uses Kidz Corner songs and growth mindset prompts at home:

1

Make "YET" a Family Habit

When someone says "I can't do this," gently add "...YET!" Make it a warm game everyone joins in on.

2

Turn Chores into Sing-Alongs

Rhythm and melody boost vocabulary retention and make routines like brushing teeth or tidying up delightful.

3

Celebrate the Effort, Not Just the Result

Praise how hard their brain worked to try again. That builds lasting confidence that stays with them in school.
