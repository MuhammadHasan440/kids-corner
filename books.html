THE TRUE STORY BEHIND THE BOOK

A Little Word. A Big Difference.

Inspired by a simple everyday moment, this story celebrates the power of perseverance, learning, and believing in what is possible.

“One day, Haven was struggling with a sentence in her reading homework. Feeling defeated, she said, ‘I can’t read it.’ I looked at her with love and added, ‘...YET!’ In that instant, her frustration turned into determination.” — Dani Egalite

Every child encounters something they can’t do — until they keep learning. Inspired by this real-life moment at home, this children's book celebrates the power of "yet" and reminds young readers that growth takes time, practice, and self-compassion.

  • ✓ Building Growth Mindset: Helps children realize that struggling is a natural, necessary part of learning.
  • ✓ Positive Parent-Child Dialogue: Gives parents a gentle, memorable phrase to defuse homework tears.
  • ✓ Lively Rhythms & Art: Designed for bedtime reading, classroom read-alouds, and early learners.

FAMILY DISCUSSION GUIDE

Questions to Explore with Your Little Learner

Use these gentle conversation starters at bedtime or over breakfast to build a growth mindset together.

⭐

What was hard that is now easy?

Remind your child of something they couldn’t do last year (like counting, riding a scooter, or buttoning a coat) to prove that practice works!

💬

What magic word can we add?

When someone catches themselves saying “I can’t,” ask them: “What’s our secret family magic word?” Encourage them to smile and shout “YET!”

🧠

How does your brain stretch?

Explain that just like our muscles get stronger when we jump, our brain grows stronger every single time we try something tricky.
